.DISCUSSION...
Issued at 1218 PM CDT Thu Oct 8 2026

The near term forecast is dominated by Hurricane Isaias. Isaias is 
tracking northeast over the central Gulf and intensifying. A 
southern stream upper trof over east Texas picks up Isaias which 
continues to lift north northeast over the central Gulf today and 
tonight and begins to turn more northward Friday. There continues 
to be some differences between the physics based global models and
the AI suites on track, but the solutions still focus on the 
northern Gulf coast for landfall. There remains no changes to 
track and intensity reasoning. Isaias is expected to approach the 
northern Gulf coast late Friday as a dangerous hurricane at time 
of landfall. No changes to tropical cyclone watches and warnings. 
Increasing swell packets and dangerous large breaking waves and 
high surf to begin arriving along area beaches Friday AM. Rip 
currents to be dangerous to potentially deadly. Weather continues 
to deteriorate going into Friday night as Isaias makes landfall. 
Could also see considerable flooding rains in repeated bands 
spiraling in off the Gulf with the main concern being focused over
southwest AL, down to the northwest FL Panhandle where a moderate
risk of excessive rains is outlooked. This will especially be 
true in any detached rainbands that set up. Considering the 
rainfall, several rivers respond by going into flood this weekend.
A few may go into moderate flood stage. Storm surge inundation of
5 to 7' possible and peaking Friday night. Coastal flooding 
issues likely to linger into Saturday. Rainband tornadoes also 
possible, especially in Isaias's eastern quadrant. 

Although Isaias lifts north of the area Saturday, forecasters will 
be watching any detached rain bands that will likely continue an 
excessive rain threat. Winds will be abating though through the day.

The remnants of Isaias phase with upper trof that ejects northeast 
up across the TN/OH River valleys Sunday with dry air expanding 
eastward over the deep south. As the upper trof continues to lift 
out, it becomes replaced by an upper ridge that amplifies over the 
southern U.S. next week. This brings a return to an extended period 
of rain-free conditions during the medium range. high temperatures 
quickly rise into the mid/upper 80s, with a few locales seeing lower 
90's. Low temperatures remaining around 60 well inland to upper 
60's/lower 70's near the coast. /10

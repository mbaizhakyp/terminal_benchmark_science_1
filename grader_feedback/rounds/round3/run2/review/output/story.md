## When and where
This message covers Alabama from 7:00 p.m. CDT Thursday, October 8, to 7:00 p.m. CDT Saturday, October 10, 2026. Flood Watches are in effect for all 67 Alabama counties in the current Mobile, Tallahassee, Birmingham, and Huntsville products.

## Flood risk
The greatest rainfall-driven flooding risk is in south Alabama, especially counties covered by WPC Moderate Risk area: Baldwin, Escambia, Conecuh, Monroe, Geneva. Using a county-area threshold of 25%, 11 counties are highest risk, with about 935,607 residents, including about 124,787 people estimated in the FEMA 100-year floodplain outside coastal VE zones. River forecasts available this evening bring the Bayou Sara at Saraland and Styx River near Elsanor gauges to moderate flood.

## Expected rainfall
South Alabama: widespread 4 to 8 inches, with local totals up to 10 to 15 inches. Central Alabama: 2 to 4 inches, locally up to 5 inches. North Alabama: 2 to 4 inches, locally 5 to 7 inches.

## Uncertainty
The main uncertainty is exactly where the heaviest rain bands set up. WPC says the higher totals should be east and northeast of Isaias' track, and repeated bands could focus flooding over a smaller corridor.

## What to do
Never drive through flooded roads: Turn Around, Don't Drown. Know your route to higher ground, or have a plan and supplies ready if water rises. Monitor the NWS and your county emergency management agency, and follow evacuation orders from local officials.

## When and where
This message covers Alabama rainfall flooding from 7 PM CDT Thursday, October 8, to 7 PM CDT Saturday, October 10, 2026. Flood Watches are in effect statewide: south Alabama Friday afternoon into Saturday afternoon/evening, central Alabama Friday evening into Sunday morning, and north Alabama late Friday night into Sunday morning.

## Flood risk
The greatest rainfall-flood risk is across southern Alabama, especially counties in the WPC Moderate Risk area. Using a 10% county-area threshold, the highest-risk counties include Baldwin, Escambia, Conecuh, Monroe, Geneva, Clarke, Covington, Mobile, Houston, Washington, and Wilcox. About 936,000 people live in these counties; about 125,000 are estimated to live in non-coastal 100-year floodplain areas. Bayou Sara at Saraland and the Styx River near Elsanor are forecast to reach moderate flood stage.

## Expected rainfall
South Alabama: widespread 4 to 8 inches, with local 10 to 15 inch amounts possible. Central Alabama: 2 to 4 inches, locally up to 5 inches. North Alabama: 2 to 4 inches, locally 5 to 7 inches.

## Uncertainty
The main uncertainty is where the heaviest rain bands set up. Repeated bands near and east of Isaias' track could produce life-threatening flash flooding, while small shifts would move the worst corridor.

## What to do
Never drive through flooded roads: Turn Around, Don't Drown. Know your route to higher ground, or have a plan and supplies to shelter safely. Monitor the National Weather Service and your county emergency management agency, and follow evacuation orders from local officials. Mobile and Baldwin residents should also follow the official hurricane and storm surge warnings; storm surge and wind are separate from this rainfall assessment.
